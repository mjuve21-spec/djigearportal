Don't buy based on specs alone. Think about how you'll actually use the drone:

  • Professional client work? → Mavic 4 Pro or Inspire 3

  • Travel and adventure? → Mini 5 Pro or Mini 4 Pro

❓ FAQs

Do I need to register my drone?

In the US, drones over 250g require FAA registration ($5, valid 3 years). Mini series and Neo are under this limit.

What's real-world battery life?

Expect 60-75% of advertised times. Air 3S: ~30-35 min. Mavic 4 Pro: ~28-33 min. Mini series: ~22-28 min.

Which has the best camera?

Inspire 3 (8K RAW) for cinema. Mavic 4 Pro (5.1K) for consumers. Air 3S for value. Mini 5 Pro for portability.
