FAA Registration — What You Need to Know

In the United States, drones weighing more than 250 grams require FAA registration for recreational use. Registration costs $5 and is valid for three years. The DJI Mini series (Mini 3, Mini 4 Pro, Mini 5 Pro), the Neo, and the Flip are all designed to stay at or below 249 grams, keeping them below this threshold for recreational flyers.

Note that even sub-250g drones are subject to airspace rules, restricted zones, and local regulations. Always check the FAA's B4UFLY app before flying, regardless of your drone's weight. Commercial operators — anyone flying for pay — must register regardless of drone weight and obtain a Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which DJI drone has the best camera in 2026?
The DJI Mavic 4 Pro leads the consumer lineup with its Hasselblad sensor, 5.1K resolution, variable aperture, and 10-bit D-Log M color profile. For most users the DJI Air 3S delivers comparable quality at nearly half the price.
Do I need to register my DJI drone with the FAA?
For recreational use in the US, drones over 250 grams require FAA registration ($5, valid 3 years). The Mini series, Neo, and Flip are designed to stay under this threshold. Commercial operators must register regardless of weight and hold a Part 107 certificate.
Which DJI drone is best for beginners?
The DJI Neo ($199) is the easiest to start with — palm launch, no controller required, AI tracking. For beginners who want more capability, the Mini 3 ($419) offers a better camera and longer flight time while staying under 250g.
What is the longest flying DJI drone?
The DJI Air 3S leads with 45 minutes of rated flight time, followed by the Mavic 4 Pro at 43 minutes. Real-world times are typically 20-25% shorter depending on wind, temperature, and flying style.
Is the Mavic 4 Pro worth the extra cost over the Air 3S?
For professional work delivering footage to clients, yes — the Hasselblad color science and variable aperture are genuine advantages. For hobbyists and content creators, the Air 3S delivers most of the image quality at nearly half the price.
What accessories do I need for my DJI drone?
At minimum: extra batteries (1-2), a 128GB+ microSD card, and a carrying case. ND filters are useful for video shooters. Propeller guards are worth adding for beginners flying the Mini or Neo.
Can DJI drones fly in rain?
No DJI consumer drones are waterproof or rated for rain. Flying in wet conditions risks serious damage. Some DJI enterprise platforms carry an IP rating for weather resistance, but no consumer model does.
