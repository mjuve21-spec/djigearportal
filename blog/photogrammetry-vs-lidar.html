Drone Mapping Explained: Photogrammetry vs LiDAR

Drone mapping has transformed how industries collect and analyze spatial data. Two technologies dominate this space: photogrammetry and LiDAR.

Photogrammetry uses multiple overlapping photos to create accurate 3D models. LiDAR uses laser pulses to measure distances directly and generate detailed point clouds.

Accuracy, speed, and terrain type determine which method is best. Some DJI drones support both methods, offering flexible mapping solutions for construction, surveying, and agriculture.
