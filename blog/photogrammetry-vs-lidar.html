Drone Mapping Explained: Photogrammetry vs LiDAR

Drone mapping has transformed how industries collect and analyze spatial data. Two technologies dominate this space: photogrammetry and LiDAR.

What Is Photogrammetry?

Photogrammetry uses overlapping aerial images captured by drones to create accurate 2D maps and 3D models.

What Is LiDAR?

LiDAR uses laser pulses to measure distance and generate precise point cloud data, even through vegetation.

Accuracy Comparison

Photogrammetry offers high visual detail, while LiDAR provides superior elevation accuracy in complex terrain.

When to Use Photogrammetry

  • Construction monitoring
  • Open terrain mapping
  • Cost-sensitive projects

When to Use LiDAR

  • Forested or uneven terrain
  • High-precision elevation models
  • Infrastructure and utility surveys

DJI-Supported Solutions

DJI supports both mapping methods through enterprise drones and RTK-enabled platforms.

Conclusion

Choosing between photogrammetry and LiDAR depends on terrain, accuracy needs, and project goals.
