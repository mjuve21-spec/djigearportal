Advanced Features Explained

D-Log Color Grading

D-Log captures flat, ungraded footage that preserves maximum color and dynamic range information. Edit in DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, or Premiere Pro with professional-grade color correction tools. This is a feature usually found on cinema drones costing $5,000+.

Intelligent Flight Modes

Built-in cinematography modes handle complex movements automatically: tracking, hyperlapse, panorama, boomerang, and more. Perfect for creators who need professional results without complex manual operation.

Secure Transmission

20+ km transmission range with encrypted connection. Optional 4G LTE connectivity allows for remote operations and live streaming capabilities.
