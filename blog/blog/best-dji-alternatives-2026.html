Best DJI Alternatives in 2026: Is Potensic Worth It?

By DJI Gear Portal — Affiliate Blog

DJI still dominates the drone market, but between import uncertainty around new DJI models in the US and simple budget constraints, a lot of pilots are asking the same question: what's actually worth buying if it isn't DJI? One name comes up constantly in that conversation — Potensic.

Potensic has built a real reputation over the past couple of years, with coverage from outlets like TechRadar, PCMag, Tom's Guide, and Yahoo, largely because it delivers DJI-adjacent features — 4K video, active tracking, gimbal stabilization — at a noticeably lower price point. Here's how their current ATOM lineup stacks up.

Potensic ATOM 3 — The New Flagship

Potensic's newest release pushes into territory that used to be DJI-only: 4K/60fps video, 8K photo capture, transmission range up to 16km, and roughly 50 minutes of flight time on a single charge. It also stays under the 249g weight threshold, which keeps registration requirements lighter in most regions — the same appeal that makes DJI's Mini series so popular. If you want the newest tech Potensic has without stepping up to DJI pricing, this is the one to look at.

Potensic ATOM 2 — The Proven Pick

This is Potensic's most reviewed and most talked-about drone, and it's easy to see why. It shoots smooth 4K/30fps video with 48MP stills, uses a 3-axis gimbal for stability, and includes AI subject tracking that keeps a moving target in frame automatically. It's been called one of the best budget camera drones available by multiple tech outlets, and at a price well under most DJI models, it's a genuinely strong entry point for someone who wants real capability without the higher cost.

Potensic ATOM SE — The Budget Entry Point

The most affordable option in the lineup, built for someone who wants to try flying without a big upfront investment. It trades some of the higher-end features of the ATOM 2 and ATOM 3 for a lower price, making it a reasonable first drone for testing whether flying is something you'll stick with before spending more.

DJI vs. Potensic: Who Should Choose What

Choose DJI if: you want the most refined flight experience, the largest ecosystem of accessories and support, and don't mind paying a premium for it. DJI's camera quality, obstacle avoidance, and app polish are still generally considered the industry benchmark.

Choose Potensic if: budget is the main factor, you're new to flying and don't want to risk a high-cost drone while learning, or you want a capable secondary drone alongside a DJI setup for lower-stakes flights.

Bottom Line

Potensic isn't trying to dethrone DJI — it's carving out the budget-conscious and beginner segment DJI doesn't always serve well at lower price points. If cost is your main constraint right now, or you simply want a second, lower-risk drone in your bag, the ATOM lineup is a legitimately solid option, not just a cheap knockoff.
